Who We Are

Absahnaz Care Foundation is a proposed charitable initiative focused on helping vulnerable children access formal education.

◎

Our Mission

To support school enrollment, learning materials, educational sponsorship, and partnerships with families and schools.

☆

Our Vision

A society where every child has access to education and the opportunity to build a better future.

Our Programs

How We Aim to Make a Difference

Our first priority is to help children facing barriers to education, starting with a small, carefully supported project in Damaturu.

✎

Back-to-School Initiative

Helping out-of-school children explore a safe and supported return to formal education.

▤

Almajiri Education Support

Supporting children who wish to pursue formal education in collaboration with their families and appropriate schools.

▣

Educational Sponsorship

Working toward support for school fees, uniforms, books, and essential learning materials.

♡

Child Welfare

Promoting the safety, dignity, privacy, and well-being of children participating in our programs.

Our First Goal

Small Steps. Brighter Futures.

We aim to identify and assess five children in Damaturu, Yobe State, and build a realistic plan to support their education. Support will depend on available funding and each child's needs.

5Children: initial target
1Starting location: Yobe
