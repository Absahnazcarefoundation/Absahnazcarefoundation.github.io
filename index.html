About Us

Absahnaz Care Foundation is an initiative dedicated to supporting vulnerable children and helping them build a brighter future. We believe every child deserves access to education, essential welfare and opportunities to succeed.

Founder: Pharm. Abdurrahman Abubakar

Location: Sabon Fegi, Damaturu, Yobe State, Nigeria.

Our Mission

To support vulnerable children through educational sponsorship, school materials, basic welfare assistance and opportunities for a better future.

Our Vision

A future where every vulnerable child has access to education, essential welfare and the opportunity to achieve their dreams.

Our Programmes

Educational Support

Helping vulnerable children access educational opportunities.

School Materials

Supporting children with essential learning materials.

Child Welfare

Providing basic welfare support where resources permit.

We aim to support orphans, Almajiri children, children involved in street hawking and other vulnerable children.
